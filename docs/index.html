PERSONAL · NON-COMMERCIAL · SELF-MANAGED

A simple connection between a personal device and Google Drive.

Ahe Mobile Automation is a private-use data transfer workflow. Its owner uses rclone to move selected, locally generated files from an Android device to the owner's own Google Drive account.

Read the privacy policy →
01

What it does

Transfers selected personal files, such as device-generated activity summaries, into folders controlled by the owner in Google Drive.

02

How access works

The owner signs in through Google OAuth. rclone uses the granted Google Drive permissions for the owner's chosen sync operation.

03

Where data stays

Transfers run on the owner's device. The website does not receive file contents or OAuth credentials and has no account registration, analytics, or advertising.

Access is owner-controlled

This is not a public upload service or a product offered to other users. It is a personal configuration of open-source rclone with Google Drive. The owner can revoke the application's access from their Google Account.
